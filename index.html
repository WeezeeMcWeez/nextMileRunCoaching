Meet Your Coach

Corrinne Ward, RD, LD

Registered Dietitian Certified McMillan Run Coach B.S. Exercise Science
"I believe everyone is a runner, regardless of pace, experience, or the path it takes to reach the finish line."

I'm Corrinne Ward, a Registered Dietitian and Certified McMillan Running Coach with a lifelong passion for helping people discover what they're truly capable of. Through curiosity, consistency, and a lot of learning along the way, running has completely changed my life.

I've developed deep expertise in both running culture and the science behind performance — from physiological adaptations and performance development to fueling, recovery, and long-term sustainability. My goal is to unlock your full potential, build your confidence, and increase your joy for running and movement.

I'm here not just as your trainer and educator, but as your cheerleader — supporting you through the challenges and celebrating your wins. When I'm not training and mentoring, I'm a full-time dietitian and mama to 2 sweet girls and 3 fluffball cats.
